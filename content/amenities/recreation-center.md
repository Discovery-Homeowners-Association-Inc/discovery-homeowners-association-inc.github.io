The Recreation Center at 8740 Stauffer Road is available to rent for birthdays,
showers, meetings, and family gatherings.

Book it through the office at least a week ahead. You will get the door code
when the rental fee and deposit are paid.

The deposit comes back after someone from the association walks through and
finds the place in good order. It is forfeited if the occupancy limit was
exceeded — that one is a fire code violation and the association has no
discretion about it.

Renters who do not own their home need written permission from their landlord;
there is a separate form for that, signed at the same time as the contract.
