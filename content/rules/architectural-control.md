If you are changing anything on the outside of your home, send the Architectural
Control Committee an application **before** the work starts.

That is the whole rule, and it is worth taking seriously in the order given.
Approval after the fact is a much harder conversation than approval beforehand,
and the committee would far rather review a drawing than send a non-compliance
letter.

## Why this exists

The ACC is six neighbors, not an office. What they are protecting is the thing
that made anyone buy here: a neighborhood that looks cared for and holds its
value. A fence that sits three feet into a common area, or a roof colour nobody
else has, costs somebody money eventually — sometimes the person who installed
it, when they try to sell.

Most applications are approved. The ones that are not usually turn out to be
misunderstandings about where a property line runs.

## How to apply

Ask the office for the current **Application for Exterior Change**, or email the
committee directly at {{< email "acc" >}}. Send it in with a sketch or a
photograph and the materials you plan to use. If you are not sure whether your
project needs approval, ask — it takes one email and saves a lot of bother.

## If you get a letter

A non-compliance letter is not a fine and it is not the end of the conversation.
It comes with a response form — fill it in and send it back. Most cases close
there.
